---
title: Book for a family member
description: Let a client add the people they book for, usually their children, book sessions for them, invite them to their own sign-in, and hand an adult their profile. Book for a family member from your server too, with attendeeClientId.
---

A client can keep a list of **family members**: the people they book for,
usually their children. Each family member is a client of the business in
their own right, with their own `clientId` (`cli_…`). The client who added
them is their **guardian**: they manage the profile and pay for the bookings.

A family member has no email, address or card of their own until they're old
enough for a sign-in. The guardian pays.

## The routes at a glance

All of these run on the client plane, with your API key and the signed-in
client's access token.

| Method | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET` | `/clients/me/dependants` | The client's family members. |
| `POST` | `/clients/me/dependants` | Add a family member. |
| `PATCH` | `/clients/me/dependants/{dependantId}` | Edit names, date of birth or notes. |
| `DELETE` | `/clients/me/dependants/{dependantId}` | Remove a family member. Past bookings stay. |
| `POST` | `/clients/me/dependants/{dependantId}/invite` | Invite a family member aged 12 or more to their own sign-in. |
| `POST` | `/clients/me/dependants/{dependantId}/release` | Hand an adult family member their own profile. |
| `GET` | `/clients/me/guardian` | For a signed-in family member: who manages their profile. |
| `DELETE` | `/clients/me/guardian` | For a signed-in adult family member: take over their own profile. |

`{dependantId}` is the family member's `clientId` from the list.

## Add a family member

```json
// POST /v1/projects/{projectId}/clients/me/dependants
{
  "firstName": "Mia",
  "lastName": "Tremblay",
  "dateOfBirth": "2014-06-15",
  "notes": "Peanut allergy; EpiPen in the blue bag."
}
```

Only the names are required. `notes`, up to 1000 characters, is for medical
and safety information. The business's staff see it; suppliers don't.

A client can have up to 50 family members. One more is `409 FAMILY_LIMIT`. A
signed-in family member can't add family of their own:
`409 CLIENT_IS_DEPENDANT`.

`PATCH` changes the names, the date of birth or the notes; `null` clears the
date of birth or the notes. `DELETE` takes them off the list, so they can't be
booked for any more. Their past bookings don't change.

The client's profile carries `familyCount`, how many family members they
manage. It is absent when they never had any.

## What a family member can do at their age

`stage` on each family member says what they can do today. Their age is worked
out in the business's time zone.

| `stage` | Age | What they can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `NO_LOGIN` | Under 12, or no date of birth | Nothing themselves. The guardian books for them. |
| `VIEW_ONLY` | 12 to 15 | With their own sign-in, see their bookings and profile. |
| `BOOKS_OWN` | 16 to 17 | Also book, cancel and reschedule. The guardian pays. |
| `ADULT` | 18 and over | Can take over their own profile. |

## Book for a family member

Pass the family member's `clientId` as `attendeeClientId` on the quote:

```json
// POST /v1/projects/{projectId}/clients/me/booking-quotes
{
  "serviceId": "srv_01kv6r83j9fmquwl81xkm5bwj6",
  "offeringId": "off_01kv6r83j9fmquwl81xkm5bwj6",
  "supplierId": "sup_01kv6r83j9fmquwl81xkm5bwj6",
  "startTime": "2026-12-09T19:00:00.000Z",
  "place": { "locationType": "VIRTUAL" },
  "attendeeClientId": "cli_01kx3n2p7q9r4s6t8v0w2y4z6a"
}
```

The family member attends, and the client still pays. The quote's
`attendeeClientId` echoes it; it is `null` when the client attends. See
[Book and pay with a quote](/guides/book-with-a-quote).

`attendeeClientId` works the same way on
`POST /clients/me/orders/{orderId}/package-bookings`, where the client's own
credits are used.

Anyone who isn't the client or one of their family members is refused:

```json
{
  "code": "ATTENDEE_NOT_ALLOWED",
  "message": "The attendee must be the client or one of their family members",
  "statusCode": 422,
  "requestId": "5c9e2a7b-3f1d-4b86-a0e4-7d2c8f1b6a53"
}
```

### From your server with the API key

The API-key booking routes take `attendeeClientId` too:

- `POST /bookings`;
- `POST /orders/{orderId}/package-bookings`;
- `POST /holds/{reservationId}/convert`.

The booking's `clientId` is still the guardian, who pays. `attendeeClientId`
must be one of that client's family members, or the call is
`422 ATTENDEE_NOT_ALLOWED`. Take the id from the client's
`GET /clients/me/dependants`.

A booking for a family member carries `attendeeClientId`. It is absent when
the client attends, and when you send the client's own `clientId`.

On the supplier plane, `GET /suppliers/me/bookings` with `expand=client` adds
`attendee.displayName` when a family member attends. The name is shown as the
service's participant settings allow. See
[Supplier portal: bookings](/guides/supplier-portal-bookings).

## When a family member signs in

### Invite them to their own sign-in

From 12, invite the family member with their own email address:

```json
// POST /v1/projects/{projectId}/clients/me/dependants/{dependantId}/invite
{
  "email": "mia.tremblay@example.com"
}
```

MarketBox sends them the business's invitation, the same one the business's
staff send. The email goes on their profile only when they accept it, signed
in with that address. The answer is the family member, with
`invitationStatus: "INVITED"`.

Sending it again re-issues the invitation. You can send one at most every 10
minutes, and 10 a day per client.

| Situation | Status and `code` |
| --- | --- |
| Under 12, or no date of birth | `422 DEPENDANT_TOO_YOUNG` |
| Another invitation is waiting for this email | `409 EMAIL_INVITATION_PENDING` |
| They already have a sign-in | `409 CLIENT_ALREADY_LINKED` |
| Sent less than 10 minutes ago | `429 INVITATION_RECENTLY_SENT` |
| 10 sent today | `429 FAMILY_INVITE_LIMIT` |

### What a signed-in family member can do

Signed in, the family member uses the same client-plane routes as any client,
limited by their `stage`:

- From 12, they see their own bookings and profile.
- From 16, they also book, cancel and reschedule. Their guardian pays, and only
  later: a card or `payWith: "NOW"` is `422 GUARDIAN_PAYS`. Where the business
  takes payment when booking, the guardian books instead:
  `422 GUARDIAN_MUST_BOOK`.
- Under 16, a booking, cancel or reschedule is refused:

```json
{
  "code": "DEPENDANT_TOO_YOUNG",
  "message": "From 16 you can book, cancel and reschedule your own appointments. Ask the family member who manages your profile.",
  "statusCode": 409,
  "requestId": "8a3d1f6c-2e9b-4c75-b0a4-1f7e5d2c9b86"
}
```

A signed-in family member sees an empty `GET /clients/me/dependants`.
`GET /clients/me/guardian` returns the client who manages their profile,
their `stage`, and `canTakeOver`. For a client who manages their own profile,
`data` is `null`.

## When a family member turns 18

From 18, the family member can manage their own profile. Either side can end
the link:

- **The guardian releases them** with
  `POST /clients/me/dependants/{dependantId}/release`. The family member needs
  an email of their own first, through an invitation: otherwise it is
  `409 DEPENDANT_HAS_NO_EMAIL`.
- **The family member takes over** with `DELETE /clients/me/guardian`, signed
  in. Show this when `canTakeOver` is `true`.

Before 18, or without a date of birth, both are `422 DEPENDANT_NOT_ADULT`.

Afterwards they book and pay for themselves. The bookings made for them so far
stay with the guardian, who paid for them, and leave the family member's own
list. The notes and date of birth the guardian kept are cleared.

### Errors and edge cases

| Scenario | `code` | Status | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Not one of this client's family members | — | `404` | Re-read `GET /clients/me/dependants`. |
| An edit raced another one | `CONFLICT` | `409` | Read the family member again and retry. |
| The attendee isn't the client or one of their family | `ATTENDEE_NOT_ALLOWED` | `422` | Pass a `clientId` from the client's family list. |

See [Errors](/concepts/errors) for the envelope.
