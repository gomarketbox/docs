---
title: Let clients manage their own bookings
description: On the client plane, a signed-in client lists their bookings and orders, moves a booking, cancels one after reading what the cancel will do, and rates the supplier, under the business's own rules.
---

A signed-in client can look after their own bookings through the **client
plane**. Your BFF sends your API key and the client's access token, and
MarketBox works out who the client is from the token. These routes check
everything your server used to check by hand:

- the booking or order is the client's;
- the service lets clients reschedule or cancel;
- the notice windows and the business's late-cancel rule.

A booking or order that isn't the client's answers `404`, the same as one that
doesn't exist. Your BFF never needs a `clientId` from the browser.

This guide assumes hosted sign-in and the
[BFF pattern](/guides/booking-flow-auth#the-bff-pattern) are working, and that
the client is onboarded with `POST /clients/me`. To book new time, see
[Book and pay with a quote](/guides/book-with-a-quote). To book for a child,
see [Book for a family member](/guides/book-for-a-family-member).

## The routes at a glance

| Method | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET` | `/clients/me` | The client's profile and saved addresses. Writes nothing. |
| `GET` | `/clients/me/bookings` | The client's bookings in a window, each with `viewerActions`. |
| `GET` | `/clients/me/bookings/{bookingId}` | One booking, with its service lines and participants. |
| `PATCH` | `/clients/me/bookings/{bookingId}` | Move the booking to another start time. |
| `GET` | `/clients/me/bookings/{bookingId}/cancel-quote` | What a cancel would do now. Writes nothing. |
| `POST` | `/clients/me/bookings/{bookingId}/cancel` | Cancel the booking. |
| `POST` | `/clients/me/bookings/{bookingId}/reviews` | Rate the supplier of a booking that has ended. |
| `GET` | `/clients/me/orders` | The client's orders, newest first. Paginated. |
| `GET` | `/clients/me/orders/{orderId}` | One order, with its lines and what is still due. |

Each path is relative to the project: `GET /clients/me/bookings` is
`GET /v1/projects/{projectId}/clients/me/bookings`. Every route takes both
credentials: `x-api-key` from your server, and `Authorization: Bearer` with the
client's access token.

Onboard the client with `POST /clients/me` first. Before that, a new person
has no membership of the project and gets `403 FORBIDDEN`. A member with no
client profile here, such as staff, gets `404`.

## Read the profile

`GET /clients/me` returns the client's own profile: name, email, phone,
`defaultLocale`, `invitationStatus`, `defaultAddressId` and `addresses[]`. It
writes nothing.

It returns less than `POST /clients/me` does. Business-only fields are left
out, and so is `lastPayment`. To show the card the client last paid with, keep
calling `POST /clients/me`, or list their saved cards with
`GET /clients/me/payment-methods`. See
[Pay with a saved card](/guides/book-with-a-quote#pay-with-a-saved-card).

## List bookings

`GET /clients/me/bookings` returns every booking of the client whose start is
in the window, in one response. It isn't paginated, so keep the window short.

```bash
curl "$MARKETBOX_API_URL/v1/projects/$MARKETBOX_PROJECT_ID/clients/me/bookings?startDate=2026-12-01T00:00:00Z&endDate=2026-12-31T23:59:59Z&status=SCHEDULED&expand=services,location" \
  -H "x-api-key: $MARKETBOX_API_KEY" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <accessToken>"
```

| Parameter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `startDate`, `endDate` | The window, as UTC instants. Both are required with any `expand` value except `client`, at most 93 days apart. |
| `status` | Only bookings in this status. |
| `expand` | Any of `client`, `services`, `package`, `location`, `participants`, `myReport`, comma-separated. Each adds one key to every row. An unknown value is `400`. |

With `participants`, a group booking shows only the client's own row. A
private booking shows every participant the client registered.

### Show only the actions that are allowed

Each row carries `viewerActions`:

```json
"viewerActions": {
  "canCancel": true,
  "canReschedule": false,
  "blockedReason": "Rescheduling closes 24 h before the session."
}
```

`canCancel` and `canReschedule` follow the service's
[participant settings](/concepts/booking-engine-model#participant-settings) and
the server's clock. Show the **Cancel** and **Reschedule** buttons only when
they are `true`. When a notice window has closed one of them,
`blockedReason` says so in a sentence you can show the client. It is absent
otherwise.

`canCancel: true` doesn't promise a free cancel. A cancel inside the
business's cancellation notice may still be refused, or may use up a package
session. Ask the [cancel quote](#cancel-a-booking) before you cancel.

### One booking

`GET /clients/me/bookings/{bookingId}` returns the booking as the client sees
it: the row with `viewerActions`, its service lines and its participants. It
carries no notes and no supplier report. On a group booking, the client sees
only their own participant row.

## Move a booking

`PATCH /clients/me/bookings/{bookingId}` moves the booking to a new start. It
keeps the length, the supplier, the location and the zone.

```json
// PATCH /v1/projects/{projectId}/clients/me/bookings/{bookingId}
{
  "startTime": "2026-12-09T22:30:00.000Z"
}
```

Find the new start with the availability search for the booking's service and
supplier. See [Availability](/resources/availability). The client and the
business are emailed.

The move is refused when any of these is true:

| Situation | Status and `code` |
| --- | --- |
| The service doesn't let clients reschedule. | `403 PARTICIPANT_ACTION_DISABLED` |
| The booking isn't `SCHEDULED`. | `409 BOOKING_NOT_SCHEDULED` |
| The booking is already inside the reschedule notice window. | `409 NOTICE_WINDOW_PASSED` |
| The new start is inside the notice window. | `422 NEW_START_INSIDE_NOTICE_WINDOW` |
| The new time isn't free, is outside the supplier's hours or is blocked. | `409 SLOT_UNAVAILABLE` |
| The new time couldn't be checked. | `409 SLOT_UNVERIFIABLE` |
| The supplier's calendar is busy with other writes. | `409 OCCUPANCY_CONTENTION`. Retry. |

## Cancel a booking

A client cancel takes two calls. First ask what the cancel would do, show that
to the client, and cancel only once they agree.

### 1. Ask for the cancel quote

`GET /clients/me/bookings/{bookingId}/cancel-quote` writes nothing:

```json
{
  "data": {
    "allowed": true,
    "refusal": null,
    "late": true,
    "lateNoticeHours": 24,
    "sessionReturned": false,
    "requiresAcceptLateCancel": true,
    "refund": "NONE"
  }
}
```

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `allowed` | Whether the client may cancel online now. When `false`, `refusal.code` and `refusal.message` say why, in words you can show the client. |
| `late` | The cancel is inside the business's cancellation notice, `lateNoticeHours` before the start. |
| `sessionReturned` | Whether a package session goes back on the client's package. `null` when the booking isn't from a package. |
| `requiresAcceptLateCancel` | The cancel must carry `acceptLateCancel: true`, because the session is used up. |
| `refund` | Always `NONE`. A client cancel never refunds money. |

A refusal is answered here as `allowed: false`, not as an error. Its `code` is
one of `PARTICIPANT_ACTION_DISABLED`, `BOOKING_NOT_SCHEDULED`,
`NOTICE_WINDOW_PASSED` or `LATE_CANCEL_NOT_ALLOWED`.

### 2. Cancel

```json
// POST /v1/projects/{projectId}/clients/me/bookings/{bookingId}/cancel
{
  "reasonCode": "CLIENT_UNWELL",
  "note": "Away that week.",
  "acceptLateCancel": true
}
```

All three fields are optional. `reasonCode` is `CLIENT_REQUESTED` (the
default), `CLIENT_UNWELL` or `OTHER`. `note`, up to 1000 characters, stays on
the booking's history and is never emailed.

The server decides what happens to the session; the body can't choose:

- **Outside the cancellation notice**, a package session goes back on the
  package.
- **Inside it**, the service's `client.lateCancel` setting decides. `FORFEIT`
  cancels and uses up the session, but only with `acceptLateCancel: true`.
  `REFUSE` doesn't let the client cancel at all.

Without `acceptLateCancel: true`, a late cancel that uses up a session is
refused:

```json
{
  "code": "LATE_CANCEL_NOT_ACCEPTED",
  "message": "This booking starts within 24 h, so cancelling it now uses up the session: it does not go back on the package. Send acceptLateCancel: true to cancel anyway.",
  "statusCode": 409,
  "requestId": "c3e8a1d4-7b2f-4a96-9e05-1f6d8b3c2a74"
}
```

Other refusals:

| Situation | Status and `code` |
| --- | --- |
| The service doesn't let clients cancel. This is off unless the business turns it on. | `403 PARTICIPANT_ACTION_DISABLED` |
| The booking isn't `SCHEDULED`. | `409 BOOKING_NOT_SCHEDULED` |
| The service's own client cancel notice, `client.cancelNoticeHours`, has passed. | `409 NOTICE_WINDOW_PASSED` |
| The cancel is late and the service refuses late cancels. | `409 LATE_CANCEL_NOT_ALLOWED` |
| The booking changed since it was read. | `409 VERSION_CONFLICT` |

**No money moves.** For a booking paid by card, MarketBox alerts the business,
which decides whether to refund it. The client and the business are emailed.

## Rate the supplier

`POST /clients/me/bookings/{bookingId}/reviews` rates the supplier who served
the booking:

```json
// POST /v1/projects/{projectId}/clients/me/bookings/{bookingId}/reviews
{
  "ratingStars": 5,
  "reviewText": "Turned up on time and was a pleasure to work with."
}
```

The rules are the same as for the review link MarketBox emails after a
booking:

- The booking must have ended. It doesn't have to be `COMPLETED`.
- A booking that was cancelled, didn't take place or has no supplier is
  `422 BOOKING_NOT_REVIEWABLE`.
- A booking is reviewed once, through either route. A second review is
  `409 REVIEW_ALREADY_EXISTS`.

The rating counts towards the supplier's average at once. It can't be edited
or withdrawn, so confirm it with the client before you send it. See
[Collect reviews and show ratings](/guides/collect-reviews-and-show-ratings).

## List orders

`GET /clients/me/orders` lists the client's orders, newest first, in every
status. Page through it with `limit` and `cursor`. See
[Pagination](/concepts/pagination).

`GET /clients/me/orders/{orderId}` returns one order with every line. Each line
has its label and quantity, and a package line has the sessions left. The money
summary has the lines, service fees, tax, total, what is paid and what is still
due.

Both are the client's view of an order. They leave out staff, attribution and
pricing-rule details.

### Errors and edge cases

| Scenario | `code` | Status | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The person hasn't onboarded in this project | `FORBIDDEN` | `403` | Call `POST /clients/me` first. A member with no client profile gets `404`. |
| The booking or order isn't the client's, or doesn't exist | `NOT_FOUND` | `404` | Show "not found". The two cases look the same on purpose. |
| An unknown `expand` value, or `expand` without a window of 93 days or less | — | `400` | Send `startDate` and `endDate`, at most 93 days apart. |
| A signed-in family member under 16 cancels or moves a booking | `DEPENDANT_TOO_YOUNG` | `409` | Their guardian does it. See [Book for a family member](/guides/book-for-a-family-member). |
| No user token, or an expired one | `HTTP_ERROR` | `401` | Sign the client in again. |

See [Errors](/concepts/errors) for the envelope.
