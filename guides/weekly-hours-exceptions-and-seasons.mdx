description: Model a supplier's real working year on the supplier plane — weekly hours, single days off, blocked time, seasonal schedules and a timezone move — and check the result with an availability search.
---

A supplier's working year is rarely one weekly pattern. They take a holiday,
block a lunch hour, work longer days in summer, and sometimes move. This guide
shows how to model each of those on the **supplier plane**, as the signed-in
supplier, and how to check what clients will be offered.

It builds on two guides:

- [Build a supplier portal](/guides/build-a-supplier-portal) covers sign-in,
  the two credentials, and the field rules for a schedule and a blocked time.
  Read [Hours and blocked time](/guides/build-a-supplier-portal#hours-and-blocked-time)
  first.
- [Set up a mobile supplier's service area](/guides/set-up-a-mobile-supplier-service-area)
  covers travel zones. Everything below applies to a schedule that names a
  zone, too.

Paths are relative to `/v1/projects/{projectId}/suppliers/me`. Every call takes
your API key and the supplier's access token.

## How availability is assembled

For each supplier, MarketBox works out open time on every request, from the
rows you write here:

1. **Schedules.** Each `ACTIVE` schedule that serves the
   [offering](/resources/services#offerings) at the requested place is
   expanded, day by day, in its own `timezone`.
2. **Minus `exdates`.** A date in a schedule's `exdates` removes that
   schedule's hours on that date.
3. **Minus blocked time.** Every `ACTIVE` blocked time is expanded the same way
   and subtracted, whatever schedule it overlaps.
4. **Minus bookings.** Every booking of the supplier that isn't `CANCELLED`
   occupies its time.
5. **Minus active holds.** A [hold](/resources/holds) occupies time exactly
   like a booking until it lapses or converts.

Busy events from a connected Google Calendar are subtracted too. Travel time
and buffers around bookings are applied on top. `INACTIVE` and deleted rows are
ignored. None of these steps is cached, so a change to hours or blocked time
shows in the next availability search.

## How `rrule` works here

Every schedule and blocked time carries a weekly rule, such as
`FREQ=WEEKLY;INTERVAL=1;BYDAY=MO,WE`. It isn't a full RFC 5545 engine. The
rule must be `FREQ=WEEKLY` with no `DTSTART`, and only three parts are read:

| Part | What it does here |
| --- | --- |
| `INTERVAL` | Every *n*th week. Weeks are counted from the Monday of the week that contains `startDate`. `INTERVAL=2` with a `startDate` of Wednesday 2026-11-04 works the week of Monday 2026-11-02, skips the next, and so on. |
| `BYDAY` | The weekdays the rule allows. Leave it out to allow every day. |
| `UNTIL` | A last date. The row stops at `UNTIL` or `endDate`, whichever comes first. Prefer `endDate`, so the row's last day lives in one field. |

**Other parts are accepted and ignored.** `COUNT` isn't read. An ordinal in
`BYDAY` is dropped: `BYDAY=1SA` saves without an error and is offered every
Saturday.

The row's own fields carry the rest. `startDate` and `endDate` bound it, as
`YYYY-MM-DD` dates in its `timezone`. Each range in `weeklyTimeRanges` names
its own `dayOfWeek` and `HH:MM` times.

**`BYDAY` filters the ranges.** A range whose `dayOfWeek` isn't in `BYDAY` is
never offered, and nothing warns you. Keep the two in step: when you add a
Friday range, add `FR` to `BYDAY`.

**Times are wall-clock times.** `09:00` is 09:00 in the row's `timezone` on
every date, on either side of a daylight-saving change. A range with
`crossesMidnight: true` ends on the next day, and belongs to the day it starts.

Any other `FREQ`, or a rule with `DTSTART`, is refused before anything is
saved:

```json
{
  "code": "VALIDATION_ERROR",
  "message": "rrule: rrule must be FREQ=WEEKLY without DTSTART (e.g. FREQ=WEEKLY;INTERVAL=1;BYDAY=MO)",
  "statusCode": 400,
  "requestId": "6c1e8a3f-2b7d-4f95-a0c4-9e3d5b7f1a28"
}
```

A monthly pattern, such as "the first Saturday", has no rule here. Model it as
a weekly rule plus `exdates` for the Saturdays they don't work, or as a
schedule per date range.

## Exceptions: `exdates` or blocked time

Two tools take time out of a schedule. They do different jobs:

| | `exdates` on a schedule | A blocked time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Removes | That schedule's hours, for whole dates | Any time, a whole day or part of one |
| Applies to | One schedule | Every schedule of the supplier |
| Lives | Inside the schedule, as a list | As its own row, with `notes`, which you list, change and delete |
| Use it for | A date this pattern never runs, such as a public holiday the business closes | Time off: a vacation, a dentist visit, a lunch break |

A rule of thumb: if the reason is the **pattern** ("we don't run Tuesday
lessons on Christmas Eve"), use `exdates`. If the reason is the **supplier**
("I'm away"), use blocked time. A supplier with three schedules blocks a
vacation once, not three times.

### Skip a date with `exdates`

`exdates` is replaced whole on `PATCH`. Read the schedule, add the date to the
list you got back, and send the full list with the row's `version`:

```json
// PATCH /v1/projects/{projectId}/suppliers/me/schedules/sch_01kv60qh3m7r2p9t4w6x8y0zab
{
  "exdates": ["2026-12-24", "2026-12-25", "2027-01-01"],
  "version": 4
}
```

An exdate removes the hours that **start** on that date. For a range with
`crossesMidnight: true`, the whole shift goes, including the part after
midnight. An overnight shift that starts the evening before the exdate still
runs into it.

### Block time off

A week of vacation, every day blocked in full:

```json
// POST /v1/projects/{projectId}/suppliers/me/blocked-times
{
  "startDate": "2026-12-21",
  "endDate": "2026-12-27",
  "rrule": "FREQ=WEEKLY;INTERVAL=1",
  "allDay": true,
  "notes": "Vacation"
}
```

The `201` returns the row in `data`, with its `blockedTimeId` (a `blk_` id)
and `version: 1`. `allDay: true` blocks from local midnight to the next local
midnight, so a daylight-saving day is blocked in full.

Part of one afternoon:

```json
// POST /v1/projects/{projectId}/suppliers/me/blocked-times
{
  "startDate": "2026-11-18",
  "endDate": "2026-11-18",
  "rrule": "FREQ=WEEKLY;INTERVAL=1",
  "allDay": false,
  "timeRanges": [
    { "startTime": "14:00", "endTime": "16:30", "crossesMidnight": false, "sortOrder": 0 }
  ],
  "notes": "Dentist"
}
```

A range in `timeRanges` with no `dayOfWeek` applies on every day the rule
allows. Give it a `dayOfWeek` to block, say, Monday mornings only.

Each shape refuses the other's fields. `allDay: true` with ranges is a `400`:

```json
{
  "code": "VALIDATION_ERROR",
  "message": "timeRanges: timeRanges must be empty when allDay is true",
  "statusCode": 400,
  "requestId": "3b9f2d6a-7e1c-4a58-9d03-5c8e1f4a7b62"
}
```

`allDay: false` with no ranges is `timeRanges must contain at least one range
when allDay is false`.

<Warning>
**Blocking time doesn't touch bookings already in it.** A blocked time, an
exdate or a shorter schedule stops new bookings. A booking that already sits
in that time stays booked, and the client still expects the supplier. Before
you save time off, list the supplier's bookings in that range with
`GET /suppliers/me/bookings?startDate=…&endDate=…`, and move or cancel them. See
[Cancel and reschedule](/guides/build-a-supplier-portal#cancel-and-reschedule).
</Warning>

To end a block early, `PATCH` its `endDate`. To remove it, `DELETE` it (`204`).
A stale `version` on a blocked time is `409 VERSION_CONFLICT` with
`"Blocked time was modified by another writer"`.

## Seasons

A season is a schedule with a date range. A supplier who works longer days in
summer has two schedules: one for the school year and one for summer, with
dates that don't overlap. Both are `ACTIVE` all year. Each offers hours only
between its own `startDate` and `endDate`.

<Steps>
<Step title="End the current season on its last day">
`PATCH` the current schedule's `endDate` to the day before the new season
starts, for example `"endDate": "2027-06-27"`, with its `version`. Leave it
`ACTIVE`: after its `endDate` it offers nothing. Do this first. While the two
date ranges overlap, the new season can't be switched on.
</Step>

<Step title="Clone it">
`POST /schedules/{scheduleId}/clone` takes no body. It copies the schedule's
hours, offerings, venues and zone into a new row, with a new `sch_` id and
`status: "INACTIVE"`:

```
POST /v1/projects/{projectId}/suppliers/me/schedules/sch_01kv60qh3m7r2p9t4w6x8y0zab/clone
```

The `201` returns the clone, at `version: 1`. An `INACTIVE` schedule offers
nothing, so the supplier can edit it without clients seeing half-made hours.
</Step>

<Step title="Edit the clone and activate it">
Change its dates and hours, and switch it on, in one `PATCH`:

```json
// PATCH /v1/projects/{projectId}/suppliers/me/schedules/sch_01kv60qhp2x7c4n9r3t5w8y6za
{
  "startDate": "2027-06-28",
  "endDate": "2027-08-27",
  "rrule": "FREQ=WEEKLY;INTERVAL=1;BYDAY=MO,TU,WE,TH,FR",
  "weeklyTimeRanges": [
    { "dayOfWeek": "MO", "startTime": "08:00", "endTime": "18:00", "crossesMidnight": false, "sortOrder": 0 },
    { "dayOfWeek": "TU", "startTime": "08:00", "endTime": "18:00", "crossesMidnight": false, "sortOrder": 0 },
    { "dayOfWeek": "WE", "startTime": "08:00", "endTime": "18:00", "crossesMidnight": false, "sortOrder": 0 },
    { "dayOfWeek": "TH", "startTime": "08:00", "endTime": "18:00", "crossesMidnight": false, "sortOrder": 0 },
    { "dayOfWeek": "FR", "startTime": "08:00", "endTime": "14:00", "crossesMidnight": false, "sortOrder": 0 }
  ],
  "status": "ACTIVE",
  "version": 1
}
```

Send the dates and `status` together. A clone has the same dates and hours
as the schedule it came from. Activating it before you change them overlaps
the original, and is refused with `409 CONFLICT`.
</Step>
</Steps>

**Don't delete a season you cloned from.** A clone names the same travel zone
as its original. Deleting a schedule whose `travelZoneRef` names one of the
supplier's own zones soft-deletes that zone too. The other season then names a
deleted zone, and quietly stops covering clients' addresses. Its venues still
work. To retire a season, let its `endDate`
pass, or set its `status` to `INACTIVE`. See
[Remove a zone, or stop working in it](/guides/set-up-a-mobile-supplier-service-area#remove-a-zone-or-stop-working-in-it).

### When a save is refused with `409`

Schedule writes have two `409`s with different fixes. **Branch on `code`, not
the status:**

| `code` | `message` | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CONFLICT` | `Schedule overlaps an existing active schedule (sch_…)` | Two `ACTIVE` schedules may not overlap in time, whichever venue or zone each names. Change the dates or hours, or make the other one `INACTIVE`. Retrying the same request fails again. |
| `VERSION_CONFLICT` | `Schedule was modified by another writer` | The row changed since you read it. Re-read it, re-apply the supplier's change, and send the new `version`. |

The overlap answer names the schedule it collides with:

```json
{
  "code": "CONFLICT",
  "message": "Schedule overlaps an existing active schedule (sch_01kv60qh3m7r2p9t4w6x8y0zab)",
  "statusCode": 409,
  "requestId": "8d4a1f7c-5e2b-4c96-b3a0-1f7e9c2d5a84"
}
```

This example adds a date to `exdates`. It builds the change from the row it
just read, so a retry merges with whatever the other writer saved:

```js
async function addExdate(scheduleId, date, attempt = 1) {
  const { data: row } = await api.get(`/suppliers/me/schedules/${scheduleId}`);
  const res = await api.patch(`/suppliers/me/schedules/${scheduleId}`, {
    exdates: [...new Set([...row.exdates, date])],
    version: row.version,
  });
  if (res.status !== 409) return res;

  const err = await res.json();
  if (err.code === 'VERSION_CONFLICT' && attempt === 1) {
    // Someone else saved first. Re-read, rebuild the change, try once more.
    return addExdate(scheduleId, date, attempt + 1);
  }
  if (err.code === 'CONFLICT') {
    // An overlap. Show the supplier which schedule it collides with.
    throw new OverlapError(err.message);
  }
  throw new SaveError(err.message);
}
```

Don't resend a stale form unchanged with a fresh `version`. That overwrites
the other writer's change, which is what `version` exists to prevent.

A few things about the overlap check:

- **Only `ACTIVE` schedules are checked.** Saving an `INACTIVE` row never
  conflicts. The check runs when it becomes `ACTIVE`.
- **Only real hours collide.** Two seasons that meet, one ending on
  2027-06-27 and the next starting on 2027-06-28, don't. A range that ends at
  12:00 and one that starts at 12:00 don't either.
- **It looks from today to a year ahead.** Hours in the past never conflict.
  An overlap more than 365 days out isn't caught when you save. Create a
  season less than a year before it starts, or check the dates yourself.

Blocked time is never checked for overlap. It may overlap anything, which is
the point.

## When the supplier's timezone moves

A schedule and a blocked time copy their `timezone` once, when they are
created: the `timezone` you send, else the supplier's profile zone, else the
project's default. After that, the row keeps its zone. So when a supplier
changes `timezone` on their profile with `PATCH /suppliers/me`, their existing
hours don't move. Rows created later take the new zone, and the supplier ends
up with hours in two zones.

To bring every row into the profile zone, call:

```
POST /v1/projects/{projectId}/suppliers/me/availability-timezone/apply-profile
```

```json
{
  "data": {
    "timezone": "America/Vancouver",
    "schedules": 3,
    "blockedTimes": 2
  }
}
```

- It keeps **wall-clock times**. 09:00–17:00 in Toronto becomes 09:00–17:00 in
  Vancouver.
- It moves `INACTIVE` rows too, so a season you switch on later is already in
  the right zone. Deleted rows stay as they were.
- The counts are the rows it rewrote. A row already in the zone isn't
  counted.
- **Each rewritten row gets a new `version`.** Re-read a schedule before you
  next `PATCH` it.
- A row someone edits at the same moment is skipped, and not counted. Call it
  again: it's safe to repeat, and moves only what's left.
- It doesn't re-run the overlap check.
- You can't choose the zone. When the profile has none, it uses the project's
  default. When neither has one, it changes nothing:

```json
{
  "code": "BAD_REQUEST",
  "message": "A timezone is required: neither the supplier nor the project has one to move the rows to",
  "statusCode": 400,
  "requestId": "2e7c5a9d-1f3b-4d68-8a04-6b9e2c7f3d15"
}
```

**Bookings don't move.** A booking is a fixed instant with its own
`bookingTz`. A booking at 10:00 Toronto time stays at that instant, which is
07:00 in Vancouver, and may now sit outside the supplier's hours. After a move,
list the supplier's upcoming bookings and reschedule any that no longer fit.

**A season somewhere else** doesn't need a profile change. Send `timezone` on
that season's schedule, for example `"America/Vancouver"` for a winter spent
on the west coast, and leave the profile as it is. Each row expands in its own
zone.

## Check the result

Open times are what clients are offered, so check them, not just the rows.
`POST /v1/projects/{projectId}/availability/suppliers` takes the API key
alone, so your server can run it after the supplier saves:

```json
// POST /v1/projects/{projectId}/availability/suppliers
{
  "serviceId": "srv_01kv60qg12e82acr78edfnjhvv",
  "offeringId": "off_01kv60qge5eh59ap0gw0pqrrys",
  "locationId": "loc_01kv60qg4h2n7r9t3w5x8y6zab",
  "lookAheadStartDate": "2026-12-21T00:00:00-05:00",
  "lookAheadDays": 14,
  "timezone": "America/Toronto"
}
```

For a mobile supplier, send the client's `lat` and `lng` instead of
`locationId`.

Each entry in `data` holds a `supplier` and their open `days`. Find the
supplier by `supplier.supplierId`, following `nextCursor` if they're not on
the first page. Trimmed:

```json
{
  "data": [
    {
      "supplier": { "supplierId": "sup_01kv60qg8c3m5p7r9t2w4x6yza" },
      "days": [
        {
          "date": "2026-12-28",
          "slots": [
            {
              "startUtc": "2026-12-28T14:00:00.000Z",
              "startLocal": "2026-12-28T09:00:00-05:00",
              "endUtc": "2026-12-28T15:00:00.000Z",
              "endLocal": "2026-12-28T10:00:00-05:00"
            }
          ]
        }
      ],
      "position": 0
    }
  ],
  "searchId": "3a8f1c6e-2d4b-4e97-b5a0-7c9e1f3d6b28",
  "timezone": "America/Toronto",
  "nextCursor": null,
  "errors": []
}
```

Read it against what you expect:

- **No 2026-12-21 to 2026-12-27 in `days`.** The vacation blocked time
  worked. A supplier with no open time on a date has no entry for it.
- **`days: []`.** The supplier matches the service and place, but has no open
  time in the window. Check the schedule's dates, `BYDAY`, `status`, and that
  the offering is in its `serviceOfferingIds`.
- **The supplier isn't in `data` at all.** The supplier doesn't serve this
  offering at this place, or search hasn't caught up with a schedule saved a
  moment ago. Which suppliers are returned comes from a search index that
  MarketBox updates shortly after a schedule is saved. Their open times are
  worked out live. Allow a short delay before you decide a new schedule
  failed.

the full request and response.

### Endpoints used in this guide

| Endpoint | Method | Auth | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/v1/projects/{projectId}/suppliers/me/schedules` | `GET`, `POST` | `x-api-key` + supplier `Bearer` | List (`includeInactive`, `includeDeleted`) and create working hours. |
| `/v1/projects/{projectId}/suppliers/me/schedules/{scheduleId}` | `GET`, `PATCH`, `DELETE` | `x-api-key` + supplier `Bearer` | Read, change (send `version`), delete. |

| `/v1/projects/{projectId}/suppliers/me/blocked-times` | `GET`, `POST` | `x-api-key` + supplier `Bearer` | List (`includeInactive`, `includeDeleted`) and create blocked time. |
| `/v1/projects/{projectId}/suppliers/me/blocked-times/{blockedTimeId}` | `GET`, `PATCH`, `DELETE` | `x-api-key` + supplier `Bearer` | Read, change (send `version`), delete. |
| `/v1/projects/{projectId}/suppliers/me/availability-timezone/apply-profile` | `POST` | `x-api-key` + supplier `Bearer` | Move every schedule and blocked time into the profile zone. |
| `/v1/projects/{projectId}/suppliers/me/bookings` | `GET` | `x-api-key` + supplier `Bearer` | The supplier's bookings in a window, to check before time off. |

### Errors and edge cases

A `VALIDATION_ERROR` message starts with the field's path, such as
`weeklyTimeRanges.0.crossesMidnight: `. The messages below are what follows
it.

| Scenario | `code` | Status | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A rule other than `FREQ=WEEKLY`, or one with `DTSTART` | `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | Send a weekly rule. Put the dates in `startDate` and `endDate`. |
| `endDate` before `startDate` | `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | `endDate: endDate must be on or after startDate`. |
| `crossesMidnight` that doesn't match the times | `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | `crossesMidnight must be true when startTime > endTime, false otherwise`. |
| A blocked time with `allDay: true` and ranges, or `allDay: false` and none | `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | Send exactly one of the two shapes. |
| No timezone on the row, the profile or the project | `BAD_REQUEST` | `400` | Set the supplier's profile `timezone`, or send `timezone`. |
| Two `ACTIVE` schedules whose hours overlap | `CONFLICT` | `409` | Change the dates or hours, or make one `INACTIVE`. Don't retry unchanged. |
| A stale `version` on a schedule, often after `apply-profile` or a zone's shape changed | `VERSION_CONFLICT` | `409` | `Schedule was modified by another writer`. Re-read and retry. |
| A stale `version` on a blocked time | `VERSION_CONFLICT` | `409` | `Blocked time was modified by another writer`. Re-read and retry. |
| A schedule or blocked time that isn't the supplier's, or doesn't exist | `NOT_FOUND` | `404` | `Schedule not found` or `Blocked time not found`. The two cases are identical. |
| `apply-profile` with no profile or project zone | `BAD_REQUEST` | `400` | Set the supplier's profile `timezone` first. |
| A range offered on no day | — | — | Its `dayOfWeek` isn't in `BYDAY`. Add the day to the rule. |
| Time off saved, but a booking still sits in it | — | — | Blocked time doesn't move bookings. Reschedule or cancel them. |
| The other season stopped covering client addresses after a delete | — | — | Deleting a schedule deleted the travel zone both seasons shared. Create a new zone and point the season at it. |

See [Errors](/concepts/errors) for the envelope,
[Concurrency](/concepts/concurrency#optimistic-versioning-on-updates) for
`version`, and [Timezones](/concepts/timezones) for zones.
