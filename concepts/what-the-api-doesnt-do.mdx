---
title: What the API doesn't do
description: The features the public API doesn't have — webhooks, refunds, payouts, invitations, creating Locations and skills, and some supplier-plane and client-plane actions — and what to do instead of each.
---

This page lists what the public API can't do today. Each entry says what to
do instead.

Read it before you design a flow around a feature you expect to exist. If a
route isn't in the [API reference](/api-reference/introduction), it isn't
there. Don't guess at a path, and don't retry a `404` hoping a route will
appear.

## At a glance

| The API has no way to | Do this instead |
| --- | --- |
| Push changes to you (webhooks, push notifications) | [Poll](#no-webhooks-or-push-notifications) the resource that changes. |
| Refund a payment | The business refunds in the MarketBox dashboard. |
| Report a supplier's earnings, or pay them | Keep earnings and payouts in your own system. |
| Invite a supplier, or manage memberships | The business invites the supplier from the dashboard. |
| Create a Location or a skill | The business creates them in the dashboard. Your key reads them. |
| Write a supplier's hours, blocked time or travel zones with the API key | The supplier does it through your portal, on the supplier plane. |
| Let a supplier create a booking, read booking notes or connect Google Calendar | Your server books with the key. Notes and Google Calendar stay in the dashboard. |
| Let a supplier change their own `email`, `maxTravelTime` or `defaultLocale` | The business changes them, with `PUT /suppliers/{supplierId}` or in the dashboard. |
| Let a client cancel or reschedule on the client plane | Your server does it with the key, after checking the booking is the client's. |

## No webhooks or push notifications

The public API never calls you. Nothing tells your server that an order was
paid, a booking was cancelled or a hold ran out. There is no route to register
a webhook, and Stripe's events go to MarketBox, not to you.

**Instead, read the resource again.** Poll what can change on its own, at the
cadence it changes. See
[Tracking state without webhooks](/concepts/tracking-state-without-webhooks)
for what changes after your request returns, what to read, and how often.

A supplier's bell in your portal is polled too. Read
`GET /suppliers/me/alerts/summary` no more often than every 60 seconds. See
[Supplier portal: insights and alerts](/guides/supplier-portal-insights-and-alerts#alerts).

## No refunds

No route returns money to a client. Cancelling a booking or an order refunds
nothing: money already recorded stays on the order, and `amountPaid` doesn't
change.

**Instead, the business refunds in the MarketBox dashboard.** If your app
offers a refund, it sends the request to the business. See
[Cancel or reschedule a booking](/guides/cancel-or-reschedule-a-booking) and
[Order lifecycle after checkout](/guides/order-lifecycle-after-checkout).

## No supplier earnings or payouts

No route reports what a supplier earned, and none pays a supplier. Insights on
the supplier plane cover demand and search, not money.

**Instead, keep earnings and payouts in your own system.** The bookings and
orders you read with the key are the inputs:
`GET /suppliers/{supplierId}/bookings` lists one supplier's bookings, and each
order carries its amounts. Wait out the 24-hour dispute window before you
treat a `COMPLETED` booking as earned. See
[The booking lifecycle](/resources/bookings#the-booking-lifecycle).

## No invitations or memberships

The API can't invite a supplier to a project, resend an invitation, or add,
change or remove anyone's membership or roles.
`POST /suppliers` creates a supplier record, but that doesn't let the person
sign in to your portal.

**Instead, the business invites the supplier from the MarketBox dashboard.**
The supplier accepts once, in the dashboard. Accepting is the only thing that
links their sign-in to their supplier record. After that, they sign in to your
portal with the same account.

Until they accept, every supplier-plane call is refused:

```json
{
  "code": "FORBIDDEN",
  "message": "No active membership on this project",
  "statusCode": 403,
  "requestId": "6a1d3f8e-2b7c-4e95-a0d4-9c5e1b7f3a28"
}
```

See [Supplier portal: sign-in and access](/guides/supplier-portal-sign-in-and-access).

## No creating Locations or skills

Locations and skills are read-only in the public API. `GET /locations`,
`GET /locations/{locationId}` and `GET /skills` read them. Nothing creates,
edits or deletes them.

**Instead, the business creates them in the MarketBox dashboard.** Your key then
reads them, and you use their ids when you create offerings, suppliers and
bookings. A supplier's own travel zones (`stz_` ids) are the exception: the
supplier creates those through your portal. See
[Locations and skills](/resources/locations).

## The API key can't write a supplier's availability

No API-key route writes a supplier's weekly hours (schedules), blocked times or
travel zones. Those are written only on the supplier plane, under
`/suppliers/me/...`, with the API key **and** the supplier's own access token.
`GET /suppliers/{supplierId}` returns a supplier's active schedules, read-only.

**Instead, the supplier sets them through your portal.** For a supplier who
never signs in to your portal, the business sets them in the MarketBox
dashboard. See
[Supplier portal: availability](/guides/supplier-portal-availability).

## What a supplier can't do on the supplier plane

The supplier plane covers a supplier's own profile, photo, bookings, hours,
time off, travel zones, insights and alerts. It doesn't cover these:

- **Create a booking.** There's no supplier-plane route for it. Your server
  creates bookings with the API key. See
  [Create a single booking](/guides/create-a-single-booking).
- **Read booking notes.** `GET /suppliers/me/bookings/{bookingId}` leaves notes
  out, because staff write them. Your key reads them with
  `GET /bookings/{bookingId}/notes`. Agree with the business before you show
  one to a supplier.
- **Connect Google Calendar.** The supplier connects it in the MarketBox
  dashboard.

See [Not on the supplier plane yet](/guides/build-a-supplier-portal#not-on-the-supplier-plane-yet).

## A supplier can't change some of their own fields

`PATCH /suppliers/me` refuses six fields: `active`, `email`, `externalId`,
`externalData`, `maxTravelTime` and `defaultLocale`. A body that names any of
them is refused whole, whatever the value, and nothing is saved.

The refusal comes back as a `403`:

```json
{
  "code": "FIELD_NOT_SELF_EDITABLE",
  "message": "Only staff can change: email",
  "statusCode": 403,
  "requestId": "1f7c3a9e-5d2b-4e86-b0a4-7c2e9f1d6b35"
}
```

**Instead, the business changes them.** Your server can do it with
`PUT /suppliers/{supplierId}` and the API key, or the business uses the
dashboard. Leave these fields out of the supplier's own form. See
[Supplier portal: profile and photo](/guides/supplier-portal-profile-and-photo#edit-the-profile).

## A client can't cancel or reschedule on the client plane

The client plane, `/clients/me/...`, has no route to cancel or move a booking.

**Instead, your server does it with the API key:**

1. Resolve the client from their token with `POST /clients/me`, and keep the
   `clientId` it returns.
2. Read the booking with `GET /bookings/{bookingId}`.
3. Refuse when the booking's `clientId` isn't that client's.
4. Cancel with `POST /bookings/{bookingId}/status`, or move it with
   `PATCH /bookings/{bookingId}`.

Step 3 matters. The key acts for the business, so the API doesn't check that
the booking belongs to the person asking. It also skips the service's notice
window and "clients may not cancel" settings. Apply the business's rules
yourself before you call. See
[Cancel or reschedule a booking](/guides/cancel-or-reschedule-a-booking) and
[Show booking and order history](/guides/manage-a-client-account#show-booking-and-order-history).
