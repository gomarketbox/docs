---
title: Tracking state without webhooks
description: What changes on its own after you write it — payment, cancellation cascades, hold expiry, the end of a session, disputes — and what to poll for each, and how often.
---

**The public API sends no webhooks.** Stripe's events go to MarketBox, not to
you, and nothing calls your server when a booking or an order changes. To
know that something changed, you read it again.

That's simple for anything your own request changes: the response is the new
state. This page is about the rest: the changes that happen **after** your
request returns, or that someone else makes.

## What changes on its own

| What changes | What changes it | When | Read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| An order becomes `PAID` after a card payment | Stripe's webhook to MarketBox | Usually a second or two after `stripe.confirmPayment` resolves. A webhook that never arrives is caught by a nightly reconciliation. | `GET /orders/{orderId}` |
| A cancelled order's bookings become `CANCELLED` | The order-cancel cascade | Seconds after the order's status change returns | `GET /bookings/{bookingId}` for each booking |
| A hold stops blocking time | Nothing: expiry is lazy | At `heldUntil`. Its `status` doesn't change. | `GET /holds/{reservationId}`, and compare `heldUntil` with now |
| A booking becomes `AWAITING_CONFIRMATION` | An hourly sweep of ended bookings | Up to about an hour after `bookingEndUtc` | `GET /bookings?startDate=…&endDate=…` |
| A booking becomes `COMPLETED`, `CLIENT_NO_SHOW`, `SUPPLIER_NO_SHOW` or `DISPUTED` | The supplier's attendance report, a client's report, or the MarketBox dashboard | Whenever they report | `GET /bookings/{bookingId}` |
| A `COMPLETED` booking becomes `DISPUTED` | The client reports they didn't attend | Within 24 hours of `COMPLETED` | `GET /bookings/{bookingId}` |
| A booking is cancelled or moved | The supplier, from your portal or the dashboard, or the business in the dashboard | Any time | `GET /bookings?startDate=…&endDate=…` |

Two things never change on their own:

- **Nothing completes a booking.** It waits in `AWAITING_CONFIRMATION` until
  the supplier reports attendance, the business settles it, or your key sets
  an outcome. No timer completes it. See
  [The booking lifecycle](/resources/bookings#the-booking-lifecycle).
- **Package credits don't expire by themselves.** A credit's `expiresAt` is
  checked when someone tries to book it. Nothing changes on the order at that
  instant.

## An order becomes `PAID`

A card payment settles when Stripe tells MarketBox, not when the browser says
so. Poll the order:

- **During checkout**, once a second for about 15 seconds after
  `stripe.confirmPayment` resolves. That covers almost every payment. See
  [Accept payments with Stripe](/guides/accept-payments-with-stripe).
- **After that**, keep the `orderId` on your server and check it again: every
  few minutes for the first hour, then hourly for a day. A payment whose
  webhook was missed is settled by MarketBox's nightly reconciliation, so it
  can take up to a day.

`GET /transactions?orderId=…` shows the payment itself: a `STRIPE` row that is
`PENDING` until Stripe confirms it, then `SUCCEEDED`.

A cash, bank-transfer or comp payment you record with
`POST /orders/{orderId}/transactions` settles in the same request. Its
response is already the answer.

<Warning>
**If you converted a hold before `PAID`, reconcile.** Convert doesn't check
payment. If you booked as soon as `confirmPayment` resolved, an order that
never reaches `PAID` holds a booking nobody paid for. Decide what your
business does with it, such as cancelling the booking or asking the client
to pay again, and run that from the same job that polls.
</Warning>

## A cancelled order's bookings

`POST /orders/{orderId}/status` with `CANCELLED` returns as soon as the order
is cancelled. The order's bookings are cancelled **afterwards**, by a cascade
that usually finishes within seconds. A booking you read straight after the
call can still say `SCHEDULED`.

The cascade:

- cancels `SCHEDULED` and `STARTED` bookings, and returns their package
  sessions to the order item;
- leaves `AWAITING_CONFIRMATION` and `DISPUTED` bookings alone, because those
  sessions have already happened;
- leaves settled bookings alone;
- sends the client one email listing every cancelled session, and each
  supplier one listing theirs.

It can't cancel a booking whose end has passed, even one that still reads
`SCHEDULED` because the hourly sweep hasn't reached it. That booking becomes
`AWAITING_CONFIRMATION` and waits for its outcome, like any session that
happened.

To confirm the cascade finished, read each booking the order funded. The
`bookingId`s are the ones convert or `POST /bookings` returned. For a package,
`GET /orders/{orderId}/package-bookings` lists them.

## A hold lapses

Nothing happens at `heldUntil`. The hold simply stops blocking the supplier's
time, and anyone can book it. `GET /holds/{reservationId}` can still say
`HELD` afterwards, so **compare `heldUntil` with the current time** rather
than trusting `status`.

You don't need to poll a hold. Keep it alive with the heartbeat while the
client pays, and expect `409 HOLD_EXPIRED` if you convert too late. See
[Hold slots during checkout](/guides/hold-slots-for-checkout).

## A session ends

A booking doesn't change at `bookingEndUtc`. An **hourly** sweep moves
`SCHEDULED` and `STARTED` bookings that have ended to `AWAITING_CONFIRMATION`,
so the change shows up to about an hour later.

In that gap the booking still reads `SCHEDULED` or `STARTED`, and two things
behave differently:

- **Your key can't cancel it.** Cancelling is allowed only before
  `bookingEndUtc`, or once the booking reads `AWAITING_CONFIRMATION`:

  ```json
  {
    "code": "BAD_REQUEST",
    "message": "Cannot transition booking status from SCHEDULED to CANCELLED",
    "statusCode": 400,
    "requestId": "5a9c2e7f-3b1d-4f68-a0e4-8d6b1c3f9e27"
  }
  ```

  Wait for the sweep, then cancel.
- **The supplier can still report attendance**, and that settles the booking
  directly.

Polling ended bookings more often than every 15 minutes gains you nothing,
because the source moves hourly.

## A booking is settled, or disputed

The supplier's attendance report settles a booking: `COMPLETED` when they
attended, `SUPPLIER_NO_SHOW` when they didn't. A client's report that they
didn't attend turns the booking `DISPUTED` when the supplier says they did. It
also turns a `COMPLETED` booking `DISPUTED` within **24 hours** of it
becoming `COMPLETED`, whoever completed it.

So **don't treat `COMPLETED` as final until 24 hours after its
`statusUpdatedAt`.** If you pay suppliers, or report revenue, from completed
bookings, run that 24 hours later. An operator resolves a `DISPUTED` booking
in the MarketBox console. Your key can't. See
[Attendance and disputes](/resources/bookings#attendance-and-disputes).

## How to poll

There's no "changed since" filter. Bookings and orders carry `updatedAt` and
`version`, but no list filters on them. Two patterns cover most needs:

- **Re-read the ids you're waiting on.** For a checkout, a cancellation or a
  dispute window, poll `GET /orders/{orderId}` or `GET /bookings/{bookingId}`
  until it reaches the state you expect.
- **Re-read a window, and compare.** For "what changed today", list
  `GET /bookings?startDate=…&endDate=…` and compare each booking's `version`
  with the one you stored. A higher `version` means something changed.
  `startDate` and `endDate` filter on when the session **starts**, in UTC, not
  on when it changed, so choose a window that covers the sessions you care
  about.

```ts
// A reconciler that runs every 15 minutes.
// Window: sessions that started in the past 2 days, or start in the next 30.
const from = new Date(Date.now() - 2 * 864e5).toISOString();
const to = new Date(Date.now() + 30 * 864e5).toISOString();

let cursor: string | undefined;
do {
  const url = new URL(
    `${process.env.MARKETBOX_API_URL}/v1/projects/${process.env.MARKETBOX_PROJECT_ID}/bookings`,
  );
  url.searchParams.set('startDate', from);
  url.searchParams.set('endDate', to);
  url.searchParams.set('limit', '200');
  if (cursor) url.searchParams.set('cursor', cursor);

  const res = await fetch(url, { headers: { 'x-api-key': process.env.MARKETBOX_API_KEY! } });
  const page = await res.json();
  for (const booking of page.data) {
    const known = await db.bookings.get(booking.bookingId); // your copy
    if (!known || booking.version > known.version) {
      await onBookingChanged(known, booking); // status, time, supplier…
      await db.bookings.put(booking);
    }
  }
  cursor = page.nextCursor ?? undefined;
} while (cursor);
```

Keep these in mind:

- **A read straight after a write can be a second behind.** If a value looks
  unchanged right after you changed it, read again.
- **Back off on `429`.** Wait before you retry, for as long as the
  `Retry-After` header says when there is one. See
  [Retry guidance](/concepts/errors#retry-guidance).
- **Poll by what you're waiting for.** Once a second for a checkout's payment,
  every 15 minutes for the day's bookings, hourly for disputes. Polling
  every booking every second spends your quota on nothing.

## Suggested cadence

| Waiting for | Poll | How often | Stop when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A checkout's payment | `GET /orders/{orderId}` | Every second | `PAID`, or after 15 seconds. Then hand it to the slow loop. |
| A payment that didn't settle at checkout | `GET /orders/{orderId}` | Every few minutes, then hourly | `PAID` or `CANCELLED`, or after a day. Then flag it for a person. |
| An order-cancel cascade | `GET /bookings/{bookingId}` for each | Every few seconds | No booking reads `SCHEDULED` or `STARTED` with an end still ahead. |
| Sessions ending, and being settled | `GET /bookings?startDate=…&endDate=…` | Every 15 minutes | — |
| The dispute window | `GET /bookings/{bookingId}` | Hourly | 24 hours after `statusUpdatedAt` on a `COMPLETED` booking. |
| Changes made by suppliers or the business | `GET /bookings?startDate=…&endDate=…` | Every 15 minutes | — |

MarketBox emails the client and the supplier about confirmations,
cancellations and moves. You don't need to poll in order to notify them. See
[Emails](/concepts/emails).
